Home page — five directions

Standalone mockups. Nothing here is wired to the app; resize the window to check mobile.

  • 1 · Sleeve
    A vinyl record's liner notes — paper, spruce green, brass, a serif built for reading credits. Albums lean on a shelf like sleeves.
  • 2 · Console
    A hi-fi receiver's rack panel — dark chassis, amber LED digits, VU-meter bars. Albums sit in illuminated slots.
  • 3 · Spread
    A music magazine feature — bold condensed display type, Risograph duotone (cobalt/yellow) art, a contact-sheet grid.
  • 4 · Catalog
    A library index card — typewriter type, navy ink, a rubber-stamped headline. Albums stand spine-out, one pulled forward.
  • 5 · Spotify-style
    Near-black surface, green accent, rounded shelves. The hero album cover washes its own color down the page and fades to black behind it; each shelf tile glows faintly in its own color underneath.

Album art in all five is generated on the fly from each title (see data.js) — no real covers are stored or fetched.
